# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want `stitch-me-up` to be a welcoming, practical, and respectful project for everyone involved.

Participants are expected to:

- be respectful and constructive
- assume good intent while giving direct feedback
- keep discussions focused on the project and its users
- avoid harassment, discrimination, and personal attacks

## Expected Behavior

- Use clear, specific, and professional language.
- Offer criticism of ideas and implementations, not people.
- Respect different experience levels and help others learn.
- Keep the project scoped to its stated goals unless a deliberate change is being discussed.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or abusive language
- Trolling or deliberate disruption
- Publishing private information without permission
- Repeated bad-faith arguments or hostile conduct

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and comments that violate this code of conduct.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the maintainers privately.
